RA SOCIAL AI — PHASE 2: LANGUAGE AI

Implemented:
- Automatic language detection for English, Tamil, Tanglish, Hindi, Malayalam, Telugu and Kannada.
- Explicit language requests take priority.
- Automatic language switching when the latest request changes language.
- Tanglish-aware casual style detection.
- Translation instruction handling.
- Grammar and spelling correction behavior.
- Formal/casual/professional tone preservation and switching instructions.
- Simple explanation behavior.
- Multilingual response rules that avoid unnecessary language mixing.
- Language metadata returned by the AI chat endpoint.
