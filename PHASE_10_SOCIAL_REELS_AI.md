# RA Social AI — Phase 10: Social Media + Reels AI

## Features
- Content ideas and content pillars
- Captions and social posts
- Reel scripts with hook, beats, visuals, on-screen text and CTA
- Hook generation
- Hashtags
- CTA generation
- Titles and descriptions
- Content calendars
- Engagement ideas
- Content repurposing
- Platform adaptation
- A/B variants
- Posting strategy
- Performance/metric interpretation
- Platform choices: all, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, X
- Tone controls
- Tamil/Tanglish/English and other Phase 2 language behavior
- Conversation history persistence

## Backend
POST `/api/ai/social` is protected by auth and persists user/assistant messages in AI history.

## Model policy
Uses existing Groq models only. No new paid provider and no NVIDIA dependency. Does not promise guaranteed reach, virality, or follower growth.
